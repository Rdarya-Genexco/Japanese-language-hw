Doc Translate

Delete your Doc Translate account

Applies to the Doc Translate website, desktop app and Android app.

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open doc-translate.netlify.app or the Doc Translate app and sign in with Google.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm in the "Are you sure?" popup.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, role, language and app preferences)
  • All your worksheets, translations, photos and folders
  • Classes you created, assignments, and your submissions and grades
  • Share links you created
  • Your sign-in account

Deletion is permanent and happens right away. Nothing is kept afterwards, except data may remain in routine Google Firebase backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

Can't sign in?

Email krishnav.daryanani@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Doc Translate account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.
