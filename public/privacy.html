Doc Translate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Doc Translate ("the app", "we", "us") is a web app at doc-translate.netlify.app that helps students and teachers translate English worksheets into other languages, organise them in folders, and share assignments within a class. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

From your Google account, when you sign in with Google:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture (if you have one)
  • A unique account ID issued by Google / Firebase Authentication

We only request basic profile and email access. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

Content you create or upload in the app:

  • Worksheets you upload (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, PNG or JPG files) and the translated versions the app generates
  • Folder names and how you organise your worksheets
  • Your chosen role (student or teacher)
  • Class information: class codes, the list of students in a class, assignments, submitted worksheets, comments and teacher feedback
  • Worksheets you choose to share with a public link

Stored only on your device: your language and light/dark theme preferences, saved in your browser's local storage. These never leave your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To store, display and organise your worksheets and folders
  • To translate and format your worksheets
  • To let teachers create classes and assignments, and let students join classes and submit work
  • To show your name and profile picture to your teacher or classmates within a class you join

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Services that process your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — sign-in and storage of your account data and content. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Gemini API — when you upload a worksheet, its contents are sent to Google's Gemini AI to read and translate it. Gemini API terms
  • Netlify — hosts the website. Netlify privacy policy

4. Who can see your information

  • Your worksheets and folders are private to your account.
  • If you join a class, the teacher can see your name, email, profile picture and the work you submit to that class.
  • If a teacher creates a share link, anyone with that link can view that one worksheet. Share links do not reveal your account details.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is in use. You can delete worksheets, folders and classes yourself in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children and students

Doc Translate is designed for use in education. Where students under the age required by local law use the app, they should do so with the permission and supervision of a parent, guardian or school. We do not knowingly collect more information from students than is needed to provide the service described above.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your signed-in account and, for class content, to the members of that class.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
